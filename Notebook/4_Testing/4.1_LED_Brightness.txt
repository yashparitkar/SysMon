====== 4.1 LED Brightness ======
Created Wednesday 04 December 2024

The LED are bright-enough even at 5 mA continuous.
Accounting for the array scanning delay, design at 15mA peak for all is possible for worst case condition which would mean a total of 2.19 W
While running, we can expect only 740.52 mW.
